What it does

Any RSS or Atom feed

Subscribe to blogs, newsletters and publications — anything that publishes a feed.

One merged timeline

Every subscription collapses into a single distraction-free list instead of per-site silos.

Reads offline

Entries cache on the device, so everything you have already pulled down stays readable.

Clean reading view

Article bodies render full width and readable, styled to match the app instead of the site they came from.

Light and dark

Follows the system appearance without a settings trip.

No accounts, no tracking

Nothing to sign up for. Subscriptions live on your device and go nowhere else.
