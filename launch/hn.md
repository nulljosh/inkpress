Show HN: Inkpress – Every feed you follow, in one clean list

I follow forty blogs and I was reading none of them, because they were scattered across tabs and newsletters. Inkpress is the plain version of a feed reader. Add a URL, entries show up in order, read ones cache on your Mac. No account, no algorithm deciding what you see, nothing phoned home. Web at inkpress.heyitsmejosh.com, source on GitHub.
